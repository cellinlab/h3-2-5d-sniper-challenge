# Mission Speech Prompts

Use a system voice or Voice Design. Do not clone a real person's voice.

Initial line set:

1. Briefing: `观察区域，确认目标。`
2. Scene selected: `场景已锁定。`
3. Scope opened: `保持呼吸。`
4. Warning: `目标正在搜索你。`
5. Final warning: `位置即将暴露。`
6. Hit: `命中。`
7. Success: `目标已确认。撤离。`
8. Failure: `位置暴露。`
9. Retry: `重新建立观察。`

Keep each line short. Verify intelligibility against scene ambience and music before adding more dialogue.
